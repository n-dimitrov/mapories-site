3. How your photos are processed, including AI

  • Software reads each photo's date and GPS position, groups the trip into days and places, and drops near-duplicates.
  • AI models look at your photos. Reduced-size copies, together with place names and dates, are sent through OpenRouter to AI model providers to describe what is in a photo, choose the best ones, plan chapters and write titles and captions.
  • GPS coordinates, without the photo and without anything that identifies you, are looked up in map data to find place names. Part of this uses the public OpenStreetMap services.
  • We do not use face recognition and do not build profiles of the people in your photos.
  • No decision with legal or similar effect on you is made automatically. The result is a book design that you review before ordering.
  • The AI providers process your photos and text only to answer our request. We use them under business terms that do not allow them to train their models on your content, and OpenRouter is set to route only to such providers.

4. Legal grounds

  • Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): making the book you asked for, your account, your order and delivery.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): invoices and accounting records.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, fixing errors, and measuring how the service performs in aggregate.

We send only emails about your account and your orders. There is no newsletter.

7. Cookies

  • The app sets one cookie, mapories_uid, which holds your guest or account id for up to one year. It is necessary for the service to work, so there is no consent banner.
  • The app also keeps small preferences in your browser's storage, such as the last view you used. They never leave your browser.
  • We use no analytics, advertising or social media trackers.
  • This website (the landing page) sets no cookies.
  • When you sign in with Google or pay with Stripe, you are on their pages and their own cookies apply.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • get a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • have wrong data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, including your account, photos and books;
  • ask us to restrict processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable form.

Write to office@maporiesbooks.com from the email address of your account. We answer within one month. It is free.

Two limits: we must keep order and invoice records for the period the law sets, even after deleting your account; and an account cannot be deleted while one of its books is at the printer or in the post.

You can also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection, cpdp.bg, or to the data protection authority of the country where you live.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted. Photos are stored in private storage and are reachable only through your session. Access on our side is limited to the people who run the service. If a breach affects your data, we tell you and the authority as the law requires.

10. Children

The service is for adults. We do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 16. Photos of your own children in your book are your decision as a parent.

11. Changes

When we change how data is handled, we update this page and its date. If the change is significant, account holders hear about it by email first.
